Write It Out

A page for today

"Write down the revelation and make it plain." Habakkuk 2:2 is the verse behind Write It Out. Take a sheet of paper, or print this page, and answer three questions.

  1. What happened? In plain words, the way you would tell a friend.
  2. What do I miss most today? Small things count.
  3. What do I need this week, and who can I ask? One need, one name.

Find ongoing support

Our own programs have not started yet. Until they do, these can help you find support today.

  • Find a licensed therapist

    Psychology Today's directory lets you search therapists near you and filter for grief and loss.

  • Find a therapist who takes your insurance

    Headway helps you search for therapists and psychiatrists who accept your insurance plan.

  • Therapy for Black Men

    A directory that helps Black men and men of color find a therapist.

  • Therapy for Black Girls

    A directory that helps Black women and girls find a therapist.

  • Find a GriefShare group

    Faith based grief support groups that meet in local churches.

Reel Brothers Foundation is not a clinical provider and is not affiliated with these services.
